## p5 — Player recovery

[Experiment source](../../../tools/player_recovery_experiments.py), [tests](../../../tests/test_player_recovery_experiments.py), [result](../../../artifacts/research-roadmap/p5/player-experiments.json). Generated trajectories/glyphs/supplied intervals are explicitly synthetic. The baseline invokes the actual `TemplateDetector._predict/_learn_motion` and `text_overlap` primitives, without loading a native controller.

Timestamp normalization gives zero error instead of 7.5px mean error on two ideal irregular-cadence missing observations. Both predictors have 15px mean error after a constructed stop and 30px after reversal. The candidate abstains on camera loss/long gaps and never advances identity confirmation. These selected cases support further evaluation, not runtime promotion or a new physics model. Candidate speed/displacement/horizon limits are experiment parameters only.

Morphology did not justify promotion: closing reduces the generated full-name overlap from 1.0 to about 0.866 and partial-name overlap from 0.825 to 0.736. A shared-prefix negative already scores about 0.928 in the raw overlap primitive. Actual identity fusion has additional distributed-glyph/sprite/ambiguity checks; primitive overlap is neither the production identity decision nor proof of a production false acceptance. Opening removes thin generated glyphs.

## p6 — Monsters and combat

[Assignment/suppression experiments](../../../tools/monster_association_experiment.py), [result](../../../artifacts/research-roadmap/p6/association-suppression.json), [decision policy](../../../src/maplebot/decision.py), [decision tests](../../../tests/test_decision.py).

- **B07/B08:** retain the existing tracker. It already prevents weak/predicted detections creating targets. A bounded global assignment solves one competition counterexample but both strategies switch identities at an indistinguishable crossing. This is not a validated low-confidence two-stage tracker; weak-positive association remains deferred until independently labelled weak observations exist. Never lower the global birth/attack threshold.
- **X01:** species-aware suppression keeps two genuinely overlapping species in a constructed case, but also keeps two species hypotheses for one physical sprite in a confusion negative. Same-species animation duplicates remain suppressed. This tradeoff does not justify changing production suppression; revisit with real overlap/class-confusion labels.
- **X02:** implemented conservative interval and identity checks. A hidden target's entire uncertainty interval must fit the existing horizontal range and vertical tolerance; intervals touching/crossing player X cannot select facing. Invalid coordinates/uncertainty/confidence reject. Known fresh track-ID changes release and explicitly reacquire; unknown-ID compatibility remains. The <=1s in-place policy, short facing tap, verify window, attempt cap and no hidden pursuit remain.
- **B13:** existing evidence ladder is retained and tested: requested action → submitted backend event → observed context, with hit/kill unknown. `CombatEvidence.snapshot` keeps `hit_confirmed=None`; `combat_readiness` separates tuning/dispatch from hit evidence; target disappearance remains “kill unconfirmed.” No new causal hit classifier is justified.
- **X03:** retain current conservative range/dead-zone/facing/skill timing behavior. Visual facing needs pose labels; HP bars and damage require attributable target/UI labels and other-actor negatives; projectiles need sequential launch/path/impact evidence; AOE needs per-target identity and feedback. None is supplied by the sparse p2 corpus. Those features remain deferred until their specific labels are available.

X02 changes when L01 abstains: it now releases when a known target ID changes and declines hidden attacks when uncertainty reaches a range/platform/facing boundary. Club range, potion percentages, nearest-target margin and the requested 60-second outcome are unchanged. No native outcome is claimed.

## p7 — Navigation evidence

[Failure report](../../../tools/failure_report.py) now adds report-only navigation symptoms with original reason, phase, edge, feedback/refusal evidence and **unknown causal attribution**. Stale observation, grip, trajectory/corridor, boundary, budget, focus and map-context symptoms can coexist. A dispatch record is not proof of game input delivery; a failed departure is not proof of missing input.

[Harness result](../../../artifacts/research-roadmap/p7/harness.json): 10/10 constructed scenarios passed, including first-failure release, stale/missing/focus/map faults, dropped input and recorded handoff signature. This uses modeled dynamics, not live gameplay.

The historical handoff result at `artifacts/navigation-checks/20261030-195826/result.json` was inspected. Its referenced clip lacks `decisions.jsonl`; [the attempted policy report](../../../artifacts/research-roadmap/p7/saved-failure/summary.json) records that failure. No repeated replay was attempted. [Updated report-only classification](../../../artifacts/research-roadmap/p7/saved-failure-updated.json) correctly calls it navigation/map-context while leaving displacement cause unknown. Full action-aligned held-out replay remains incomplete until the actual source clip or another suitable saved clip is available.

**X06:** preserve the current action-driven harness, corridor/landing/grip checks and physics estimator. Existing physics evidence identifies a rise constraint, not speed/acceleration/braking/gravity/takeoff speed. Synthetic success must not fill these unknowns. Route compression and additional teaching UI are deferred until a measured route-authoring/execution burden exists; any compression must preserve launch, landing, corridor and boundary constraints. New physical parameters require sufficient independent action-aligned flights and held-out validation.
